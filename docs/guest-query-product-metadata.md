# Governed PRODUCT metadata in local structured queries

Typed ITEM content with itemType PRODUCT describes that current product identity. It does
not establish a global visitor rule, sale, seller, stock, or eligibility. A description is
free text; this interpretation is an explicit publishing/data-quality contract, not a
syntactic proof or automatic prose classifier. Publish applicable rules through POLICY.

The compiler separately proves IDENTITY_SCOPED_PRODUCT_METADATA for current admitted,
unique PRODUCT identities. It requires validated publication/revision identity and does
not issue the proof for ambiguous names or products incident to unresolved relationships,
including unauthorized endpoints. This is distinct from FULLY_SCOPED place applicability.
An empty place set never becomes complete scope or proof of no sale.

Only an exact current protected-SQL Knowledge projection may be suppressed: source type,
module/revision/publication IDs, title, category, and whole body must agree with the current
published snapshot and its private prepared-bound metadata proof. Selected Knowledge is
always retained. Directly requested unique products retain whole metadata even with zero
known sellers, without adding a place row or an offer. Tainted identities keep conservative
Knowledge evidence. Missing/corrupted relevant bodies fail before output.

Unrelated exact PRODUCT metadata aliases may be omitted from a selected-record answer.
No body is truncated and no source record is deleted. The full source/version still binds
the authority. Policies, generic legacy Knowledge, unknown scopes, and policies targeting
unoffered products retain their existing conservative treatment. Policy coverage remains
UNKNOWN and published offers remain positive-only.

The direct formatter returns full omittedProductMetadata IDs/reasons for QA. Its durable
receipt contains omission count and a SHA-256 binding sourceVersion, private snapshot
identity, query identity, and the full omission proof list. Omitted aliases are not cited
as retained sources; a duplicate alias whose canonical whole metadata was retained is
labelled separately from unrelated metadata. The compiler contract changes source hashes
and invalidates cross-version cursors/receipts; there is no global mutable cache.

The local experiment stays default-off. Its existing source/native/config fences,
99-added-source allowance and 10,000-character composed answer bound remain in force.
If relevant metadata or conservative policy context exceeds a bound, decline intact.
This contract does not promise an exhaustive inventory, an exact seller total when offer
coverage is unknown, a policy eligibility decision, or global semantic correctness of
operator-authored free text.
